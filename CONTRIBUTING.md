# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. Heads-up is shared so you can use it, change it and build on it freely under its license, but it isn't open to outside contributions: pull requests are closed without review.

- **Want a change?** Fork the repo and make it yours.
- **Found a bug?** Open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened (`hook.log` in `~/Library/Application Support/heads-up` helps). Fixes aren't promised, but bug reports are read.
