    Voice

    Who speaks the alerts. Press play to hear a voice on your Mac before choosing it. You can add any voice from your ElevenLabs account or Sarvam's speakers too.

    Add a voice

    Alerts

    Each one starts with "Hello." Turn off the ones you don't want to hear, or change the words: your version is recorded in your voice with your own key.

    Automatic alerts

    These play even if Claude forgets to call the tool. Background sessions, like scripted runs and agent workers, never trigger them.

    When Claude asks for permission
    Plays Approval while a permission prompt is waiting for you.
    When a long task finishes
    Plays Task complete, or Input needed if Claude ends with a question. Skipped when Claude already spoke.
    min or longer
    Remind me when Claude is waiting
    Plays Reminder after Claude has waited on you for about a minute.

    Claude's own words

    When a fixed line doesn't say enough, Claude can speak a short line of its own, like “The migration ran, but two tables need your review.” It's recorded with your key, and the same switches, quiet times and volume caps apply. If it can't be recorded, the regular alert plays instead.

    Let Claude speak its own lines
    Lines are sent to your voice provider, so Claude is told to keep code and secrets out of them.
    Longest line
    Longer lines are cut at a word.
    characters
    Daily budget
    The most Heads-up records on its own each day, per provider: Claude's lines, project names and alerts in new voices. Set 0 to record nothing new. What you record from this page isn't capped.

    Project names

    Calls and Focus

    Stay quiet while you're on a call or have a Focus mode on. Heads-up checks device state only; it never listens or records.

    When a microphone is in use
    Zoom, Meet, FaceTime, dictation
    When a camera is in use
    Video calls and recordings
    When a Focus is on
    Focus modes you switch on, and scheduled ones

    Locations

    Limit alerts to places like home or your office. A place is recognised by the network you're on, with no GPS, and nothing leaves your Mac.

    Only play at saved locations
    Alerts stay silent on any other network.

    Limits

    Keep alerts from becoming noise.

    Only when you're away
    Stays silent if you've used the keyboard, mouse or trackpad recently.
    seconds idle
    Quiet hours
    No alerts in this window. It can run past midnight.
    to
    Gap between alerts
    If two alerts come close together, the second is skipped.
    seconds
    Hourly limit
    Stops a runaway loop from repeating alerts.
    per hour
    Chime before the voice
    A short tone so the alert doesn't start mid-word.

    Volume

    If your Mac is muted or quiet, an alert briefly turns it up, then puts it back. It never goes past the max.

    Adjust volume for alerts
    Off plays at whatever volume your Mac is set to.

    Voice provider keys

    Needed to record anything new: project names, your own wording, added voices and Claude's own lines. The built-in alerts work without a key. Keys go into your macOS Keychain and are never shown again.
